About Batlas

Batlas makes small co-op games for people who already sit together. A classroom table, a couch, a kitchen counter. The games run in the browser on iPad, iPhone and Mac. Nothing to install.

Your account is a handle, a display name and a PIN of four to six digits. We do not ask for an email. There are no ads on the site or in the games.

Sky Crew is the first game on the lineup: a short flight you work as a crew. Load the bags, look after the cabin, and land with your friends. More games will show up here when they are ready to board.

If a PIN is forgotten, a staff member can reset it and hand over a temporary one. We cannot email a reset, because the account has no email. Ask a teacher or a Batlas admin you know.
