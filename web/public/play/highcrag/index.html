A BATLAS GAME
HIGHCRAG

Your plane went down on a lonely island. The only way out is up.

Expedition
·
Climb with friends up to 4
Co-op needs a Batlas account. Sign in
How to play
  • Start at the airport holding your passport: use it (F, or ● Use on touch) to change your look. Board at Gate 7, and survive the crash on the island's beach. Grab a compass, rope and flares from the wreck.
  • Climb: hold left mouse (or R) facing a rock wall; W/S up and down, A/D sideways, Space to lunge. On touch, turn on ✋ Grab.
  • Stamina (yellow) drains while you climb. Hunger, cold and injury fill the bar from the right and shrink it. Fill it completely and you black out.
  • E picks up items and lights campfires (team checkpoints). F uses the selected item, 1–3 select, Q drops. T / G / Y wave, cheer, point.
  • Ropes: stand at a ledge edge facing the drop and use one. Anyone can climb it cheaply. Pitons: place them on a wall to rest. Magic beans grow a vine up a wall that is quick to climb.
  • Pack weight: everything you carry trims your stamina bar a little, so choose what to keep. A bad mushroom poisons you; bandages cure it.
  • Eat food next to a lit campfire to cook it (worth more; roasted marshmallows are a feast). Chalk makes climbing cheaper, a lantern keeps you warm, binoculars zoom.
  • Z pings a spot for your team; the signal horn calls everyone to you. In co-op, E gives the item in your hand to a nearby friend, and C carries a downed friend (bring them to a campfire to revive them).
  • Co-op: if a friend goes down, press E next to them to help them up. Standing above a climbing friend? Press E to pull them up.
  • Light the beacon on the summit to be rescued.
