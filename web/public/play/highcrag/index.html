Highcrag

Climb a lone island mountain from the beach to the smouldering crown. Manage your grip, keep fed, keep warm and don't look down.

How to play
  • WASD move · mouse look (click to lock) · Shift run · Space jump
  • Hold left mouse (or R) facing a rock wall to grab and climb. W/S up and down, A/D sideways. Space while climbing lunges upward.
  • Climbing drains stamina (yellow). Run out and you let go. Rest on flat ledges to recover.
  • Hunger, cold and injury eat into the bar from the right and shrink your maximum stamina. If they fill it, you black out and wake at your last campfire without your gear.
  • E picks up items and lights campfires (they become your checkpoint and warm you up). F uses the selected item, 1–3 or the mouse wheel select, Q drops.
  • Pitons can only be placed while climbing: hang next to one to get your breath back.
  • Mossgrove has thornbrush. Frostline has blizzards. The Ember Crown has fierce gusts and thin air. Light the beacon at the summit to finish.
  • Touch: left stick moves, drag on the right looks, hold ✋ to climb.
