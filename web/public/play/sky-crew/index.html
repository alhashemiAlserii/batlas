How to play

Your first steps

Stow the bags in the forward hold, board the passengers and see them into their seats, then close the rear door and the cargo door. Run the checklist: seatbelt sign, a short welcome, gear down and lights on. Take the captain's seat and lift off. In the air, answer the calls, deal with a flare or a spill if one starts, then line up, lower the gear and land.

Touch: a move stick, plus round buttons labelled Jump, Use, Run and Throw. Throw shows up while you are carrying something. In the captain's seat, hold the throttle dial and it fills as power comes on. Gear, flaps and leave sit beside it. Open Options to drag, resize or reset each control. The layout is saved on this device, separately for landscape and portrait.

Gamepad, on foot: left stick walks, right stick looks. A jump, B use, X throw, Y run. The D-pad matches the galley slots. Start pauses. Back opens the duty card.

Gamepad, in a seat: the captain's left stick pitches and steers, and the triggers add and cut throttle. A belt, B leave, X gear, Y flaps. Bumpers are lights and the announcement.
