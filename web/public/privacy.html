What we keep

Privacy

Batlas does not run ads, and an account does not need an email. We store the handle, the display name, a hash of the PIN, and game stats such as flights, stars and landings.

The PIN itself is not saved. If you forget it, a staff member can set a temporary PIN and show it once, so they can tell you in person. You choose a new PIN the next time you sign in.

Feedback sent from the button on this site is saved and visible to staff on the dashboard. It may include your handle if you are signed in, and the page you sent it from.

We do not sell this information. We do not add advertising trackers. Optional email, if you ever add one in settings, stays optional and is not required to play.

You can delete your own account from Settings. That removes the sign-in, the sessions and the flight stats tied to you. Staff audit notes stay, with your name taken off.
