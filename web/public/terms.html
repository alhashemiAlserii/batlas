The rules

Terms

Batlas is a free studio site. Opening a game means you agree to a short list of rules.

Your account

You sign in with a handle and a 4–6 digit PIN. Do not share the PIN. A display name is what friends see in a room. Staff can reset a PIN if you forget it, and they can remove an account that hurts other players.

The games

Games run in your browser. Some builds are in preview and open only for people with a preview pass, plus staff. There is nothing to buy and there are no ads. We may pause a game for maintenance.

Fair play

Don’t flood rooms, break the games on purpose, or pretend to be staff. Ranks (Developer, Admin, Trusted, Player) are checked by the server. What you see in a menu is not what decides permission.

Questions go through the feedback button. We read those notes on the staff dashboard.
